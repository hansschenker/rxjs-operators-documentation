# Reference

The reference section of this site has two parts.

- **[Operator Trees](./operator-trees/overview.md)** — a behavioral classification of the whole RxJS operator surface: every family as an invariant plus independent variant axes, the seven axes that recur across families, and decision trees that go from a requirement to an operator. Start with the [Overview](./operator-trees/overview.md), keep the [Legend](./operator-trees/legend.md) at hand, and print the [A4 Edition](./operator-trees/a4-edition.md).
- **Reference guides** — decision guides, architecture and testing patterns, framework integrations and real-world domain patterns. They are listed on the [home page](/) under *Reference Guides* and in the sidebar of any guide.

The operator pages themselves live under *Operators* in the top navigation.
